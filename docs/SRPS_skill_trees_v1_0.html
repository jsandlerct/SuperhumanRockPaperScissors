🧠 MIND — "Deduce it"
Theme: Cold analytical deduction  ·  Counters: FORTUNE  ·  Synergizes with: MYSTIC
L1 — Root
MIND.1 Mind Power Powerup Root — L1 · 5 pts 5 powerup slots. Start season with 4 Basic + 1 Advanced powerups.
L2 — Offense & Defense split
MIND.1.1 Neural Pattern Recognition Passive Offense — L2 · 10 pts +10%/round accumulating chance to read NPC throw logic at 90% accuracy. Flat 10% false result always applies — never scales.
MIND.1.2 Blank Slate Passive Defense — L2 · 10 pts NPC pattern strategies (Historian, Streaker, Mimic) treat your last 2 throws as unknown. Always active.
L3 — Offense sub-branch & Defense sub-branch
MIND.1.1.1 Neural Scan Active Offense — L3 · 15 pts On-demand 90% read of NPC throw logic. Cooldown: once every 5 matches. Player-only.
MIND.1.1.2 Desperate Clarity Passive Synergy — L3 · 15 pts Lose 2 consecutive rounds → +20% NPR permanently for match remainder. Once/match. Synergy (MYSTIC): swap season loadout to 3B + 1A + 1L.
MIND.1.2.1 Memory Wipe Active Defense — L3 · 15 pts Purges NPC throw history — NPC reverts to base strategy as if match just started. Once/match. Player-only.
MIND.1.2.2 The Cooler Passive Soft Counter — L3 · 15 pts 50% chance opponent TML/ATML fails → opponent loses round. Only on TML/ATML use. Upgrades to 75% with The Freezer.
L4 — Aspirational (largely unreachable in a single playthrough)
MIND.1.1.1.1 Neural Scan 2.0 Powerful Active — L4 · 20 pts Replaces Neural Scan. Same 90% on-demand read. Cooldown: every 3 matches (was 5). Shared UI button and tracker.
MIND.1.1.1.2 Adv. Neural Pattern Rec. Powerful Passive — L4 · 20 pts NPR per-round accumulation: 10% → 15%. Always active. Does not affect accuracy or false result chance.
MIND.1.1.2.1 Mental Mysticism Synergy Active — L4 · 20 pts Precondition: NPR fired this match. 90% tie → win. Cooldown: every 3 rounds.
MIND.1.1.2.2 Uncanny Mind Synergy Passive — L4 · 20 pts +10% tier upgrade chance / +5% Legendary chance on drops. Stacks additively with MYSTIC.1.
MIND.1.2.1.1 Total Recall Powerful Active — L4 · 20 pts Memory Wipe AND reveals the base strategy NPC reverts to. Once/match.
MIND.1.2.1.2 Clean Slate Powerful Passive — L4 · 20 pts After any purge (Memory Wipe or Total Recall): NPC pattern strategies locked out for 3 rounds.
MIND.1.2.2.1 Not Today! Hard Counter Active — L4 · 20 pts 95% chance TML/ATML fails → opponent loses round. Cooldown: every 3 rounds. Takes precedence over Cooler/Freezer.
MIND.1.2.2.2 The Freezer Hard Counter Passive — L4 · 20 pts Upgrades The Cooler: 50% → 75% block chance against TML/ATML.
🔮 MYSTIC — "Bend it"
Theme: Supernatural reality alteration  ·  Counters: MIND  ·  Synergizes with: FORTUNE
L1 — Root
MYSTIC.1 Mystical Power Powerup Root — L1 · 5 pts +15% chance of one tier higher on random drops. +5% Legendary chance. Stacks additively with Uncanny Mind.
L2 — Offense & Defense split
MYSTIC.1.1 Tweak Reality Passive Offense — L2 · 10 pts 30% chance a natural tie converts to a win. Always active. Upgrades to 60% with Alter Reality.
MYSTIC.1.2 Reverse Card Passive Defense — L2 · 10 pts 25% chance any opponent active skill fires for the MYSTIC player instead. Always active.
L3 — Offense sub-branch & Defense sub-branch
MYSTIC.1.1.1 Force Your Hand Active Offense — L3 · 15 pts 90% tie → win. No effect on non-tie rounds. Cooldown: every 5 rounds.
MYSTIC.1.1.2 Third Time's the Charm Passive Synergy — L3 · 15 pts 2 consecutive failed tie conversions → 95% next attempt. Once/match. Synergy (FORTUNE): start season with 1 Legendary powerup.
MYSTIC.1.2.1 Brain Fart Active Defense — L3 · 15 pts Nullify opponent active skill + add 2 rounds to its cooldown. Cooldown: every 3 rounds.
MYSTIC.1.2.2 Mind Shield Passive Soft Counter — L3 · 15 pts 50% block NPR / Neural Scan reads. Always active. Upgrades to 90% with Mind Fortress.
L4 — Aspirational (largely unreachable in a single playthrough)
MYSTIC.1.1.1.1 Twist Your Arm Powerful Active — L4 · 20 pts Replaces Force Your Hand. Same 90% tie → win. Cooldown: every 3 rounds (was 5).
MYSTIC.1.1.1.2 Alter Reality Powerful Passive — L4 · 20 pts Upgrades Tweak Reality: 30% tie conversion → 60%. Always active.
MYSTIC.1.1.2.1 Refuse to Lose Synergy Active — L4 · 20 pts 90% chance a loss converts to an immune tie. Cooldown: every 3 rounds.
MYSTIC.1.1.2.2 Probability Storm Synergy Passive — L4 · 20 pts 50% chance any non-Basic drop yields 2 powerups instead of 1.
MYSTIC.1.2.1.1 Massive Brain Fart Powerful Active — L4 · 20 pts Nullify + 3 rounds on target's cooldown + 1 round on all other opponent cooldowns. Every 3 rounds.
MYSTIC.1.2.1.2 Bamboozle Powerful Passive — L4 · 20 pts 25% chance any opponent powerup activates for you instead. Always active.
MYSTIC.1.2.2.1 Phantom Memory Hard Counter Active — L4 · 20 pts When Neural X fires (after block check): plant a false strategy read. 1-round cooldown.
MYSTIC.1.2.2.2 Mind Fortress Hard Counter Passive — L4 · 20 pts Upgrades Mind Shield: 50% → 90% block chance against Neural X reads.
🍀 FORTUNE — "Trust it"
Theme: Luck and probability manipulation  ·  Counters: MYSTIC  ·  Synergizes with: MIND
* FORTUNE intentionally reverses Active/Passive roles in the offense branch at L2 and L3. FORTUNE.1.1 (L2) is Active rather than Passive; FORTUNE.1.1.1 (L3) is Passive rather than Active.
L1 — Root
FORTUNE.1 Fortunate Power Powerup Root — L1 · 5 pts 2× powerup drop rate per round. (Playtesting flag — easy nerf to 1.5× if needed.)
L2 — Offense & Defense split (* offense branch is Active, not Passive)
FORTUNE.1.1 Trust My Luck (TML) Active L2* (intentional reversal) · 10 pts 75% auto-win / 25% auto-loss. Supersedes RPS throw entirely. Cooldown: every 5 rounds.
FORTUNE.1.2 Consolation Prize Passive Defense — L2 · 10 pts 30% chance to earn a Basic luck powerup on a round loss. Always active.
L3 — Offense sub-branch & Defense sub-branch (* offense sub-branch is Passive, not Active)
FORTUNE.1.1.1 Lucky Socks Passive L3* (intentional reversal) · 15 pts TML/ATML success chance: 75% → 85%. Always active. Replaced by Fingers Crossed (95%) at L4.
FORTUNE.1.1.2 Due for a Win Passive Synergy — L3 · 15 pts 2 consecutive TML/ATML failures → next attempt 95%. Once/match. Synergy (MIND): gain 6th powerup slot (starts empty each season).
FORTUNE.1.2.1 Change My Luck Active Defense — L3 · 15 pts On round loss: earn 2 Basic+ luck-themed powerups. Cooldown: every 3 rounds.
FORTUNE.1.2.2 Oblivious Passive Soft Counter — L3 · 15 pts 50% block MYSTIC tie-altering skills. Always active. Upgrades to 90% with Totes Oblivious.
L4 — Aspirational (largely unreachable in a single playthrough)
FORTUNE.1.1.1.1 Advanced TML (ATML) Powerful Active — L4 · 20 pts Replaces TML entirely. Same 75/25 odds. Cooldown: every 3 rounds (was 5). Shared UI button and tracker.
FORTUNE.1.1.1.2 Fingers Crossed Powerful Passive — L4 · 20 pts TML/ATML: 85% → 95% success chance. Replaces Lucky Socks value — does not stack.
FORTUNE.1.1.2.1 I've Got a Hunch (IGaH) Synergy Active — L4 · 20 pts 50% chance of a 90% strategy read. Not blocked by MYSTIC counters. Failure: silent fizzle. Every 3 rounds.
FORTUNE.1.1.2.2 Make Your Own Luck Synergy Passive — L4 · 20 pts On any powerup drop: system generates 2 options, player chooses 1 via blocking UI prompt.
FORTUNE.1.2.1.1 Reversal of Fortune Powerful Active — L4 · 20 pts Round loss → earn 2 Advanced+ luck-themed powerups. Cooldown: every 3 rounds.
FORTUNE.1.2.1.2 Look What I Found Powerful Passive — L4 · 20 pts On loss: 25% independent chance of powerup one tier above Consolation Prize. Both can trigger on same loss.
FORTUNE.1.2.2.1 Lucky Charm Hard Counter Active — L4 · 20 pts MYSTIC tie-alter fires → reverse result to Lucky Charm player's favor. Fires after Oblivious fails. Fizzles if no tie-alter fires. Every 3 rounds.
FORTUNE.1.2.2.2 Totes Oblivious Hard Counter Passive — L4 · 20 pts Upgrades Oblivious: 50% → 90% block chance against MYSTIC tie-altering skills.
