01  —  GAME OVERVIEW
It's 2050. Biotech implants unlocked superhuman abilities — and the world immediately used them to get better at Rock Paper Scissors. The UN adopted RPS as the universal dispute resolution framework. The World Champion holds more informal power than any head of state. You've spent your whole life dreaming of that title. Today, in a small gymnasium that smells faintly of linoleum and ambition, your journey begins.
▶  Your goal: compete across up to 5 tournaments per season, climb the global ELO rankings, and — after a 10-season career — earn induction into the Hall of Fame.

04  —  ROUND FLOW
Regular matches are Best-of-5 (first to 3). Finals matches are Best-of-7 (first to 4). Ties replay automatically unless a skill prevents it.
01 THROW SELECTION Pick ✊ ✋ ✌ via the hand buttons. Activate powerups here. Trigger one active skill (uses round's single active skill slot).
→
02 GUT CHECK Skill intel displays: reads, warnings, false reads. Certain skills let you change your throw here. Activate powerups. Use your active skill if not used in Phase 1.
→
03 RESOLUTION Both throws revealed. Winner determined. Score updated. Ties replay from Phase 1.
⚠  ONE ACTIVE SKILL PER ROUND — across both phases combined. This is the primary balance mechanism. You cannot change your throw by default during Gut Check — only specific skills or powerups grant that ability.

SKILL POINTS ECONOMY
  • Start Season 1 with 15 free pts (Jessie's training)
  • Earn pts by winning or placing 2nd in each tournament
  • Mid-season: add pts only — no removal until off-season
  • Off-season: full respec within your two trees
  • Tree identity (which two) is locked for the entire playthrough
  • Must purchase at least one node before starting any season
  • Perfect 10-season run ≈ 1,015 total points — enough for ~4–5 L4 nodes
🔗  CROSS-TREE SYNERGIES: Each tree's L3 Synergy node (X.1.1.2) unlocks a bonus if you have any points in the synergy partner tree. Example: MIND's Desperate Clarity swaps your season loadout to include a Legendary powerup if you also have MYSTIC points.
06  —  POWERUP SYSTEM
TIERS
  • BASIC — small advantage, longer duration
  • ADVANCED — moderate, balanced scope
  • LEGENDARY — game-changing, dramatic gold animation
SLOTS & DROPS
  • Default: 3 slots. MIND.1 → 5 slots. FORTUNE synergy → 6 slots
  • Powerups drop on round wins (10% → 20% → 40% → 80% → 160%+ escalation)
  • 10% base chance to upgrade one tier on any drop
  • Overflow: blocking prompt — replace or discard (no timeout)
SCOPE
  • ROUND — one throw only
  • MATCH — rest of current match
  • TOURNAMENT — rest of current tournament
  • SEASON — rare; Legendary tier only
  • Shorter scope = stronger effect within its tier
Starting loadout is deterministic (no RNG). MYSTIC.1's upgrade bonus applies to in-season drops only — not the starting loadout. Each tree gets thematically linked powerups. The universal powerup Changed My Mind (Basic, Round) lets any player change their throw during Gut Check.
07  —  ELO RANKINGS & HALL OF FAME
ELO is the same rating system used in competitive chess. Every player — you and all 99 NPCs — starts with a baseline score. When you win, your rating goes up; when you lose, it goes down. The key wrinkle: the size of the adjustment depends on who you beat. Defeating a higher-rated rival earns you more points than beating someone ranked below you, and losing to a lower-rated opponent costs you more than losing to someone at the top. This makes every match meaningful — playing up rewards you, playing safe barely moves the needle.
GLOBAL RANKINGS
  • All 100 players (you + 99 NPCs) share one ELO pool
  • Ratings update at the end of each season based on tournament results
  • NPC vs NPC matches are simulated each season — the world keeps moving whether you win or not
  • Milestones: enter rankings → Top 50 → Top 20 → Top 10 → Top 3 → #1
🏆 HALL OF FAME
  • Evaluated after your 10th season ends
  • Eligible if you rank in the Top 3 of cumulative ELO across all 10 seasons
  • Permanent HOF plaque on your Career Summary screen
  • The ultimate achievement of any playthrough
08  —  TIPS FOR NEW PLAYERS
GETTING STARTED
  • Always buy the Root node (L1, 5 pts) first — it seeds your starting powerup loadout
  • You cannot start a season without purchasing at least one node
  • Read the Gut Check messages — even false reads contain clues about the NPC's strategy
  • Powerups escalate fast: winning the 5th round gives 1–2 guaranteed drops
  • FORTUNE's Trust My Luck (75/25) is high variance — use The Cooler to punish opponents using it
